NOVADAY: STAR JOURNAL

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026

In short: Novaday works offline, needs no account, and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs. Everything you write and add stays on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer.

What stays on your device

Novaday stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your journal entries and their text.
  • Photos and videos you attach to entries.
  • Coarse location, if you allow it. It is used only to label an entry with its place or region, and is saved with that entry. Novaday does not track your location in the background.
  • Your passcode, if you set one. Only a hash of it is kept, in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore. The passcode itself is never stored.
  • App settings and a small local error log, used to show diagnostics if you choose to send feedback.

What we collect

Nothing. Novaday has no account system and no servers of its own, and it does not send your entries, media, location or any usage data to the developer or to third parties. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.

Backups

You can export a backup of your journal as a .zip file. The file is created on your device and leaves it only when you choose to save, export or share it, for example to Files, iCloud Drive, AirDrop or another app. Where it goes after that is up to you and the service you pick.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, Novaday opens either your own mail app or a GitHub issue page with a draft message. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself, and you can review or edit the message first. The draft can include basic diagnostics (app version, system version, language, screen size, number of entries and recent error messages); you can turn this off before sending. Messages you send are handled by your mail provider or by GitHub under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

Novaday asks for access to your photos, camera or location only when you use a feature that needs it, and for notification permission only if you turn on reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it. You can delete entries in the app, and deleting the app removes its data from your device. Backup files you exported are not affected and can be deleted wherever you saved them.

Children

Novaday does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at github.com/dominicshing/novaday-pages/issues.

隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 26 日

簡單來說：Novaday 可以離線使用，不需要帳號，也沒有任何分析、廣告或當機回報 SDK。你寫下和加入的一切都留在你的裝置上，不會傳送任何資料給開發者。

留在你裝置上的資料

以下資料只會儲存在你的裝置上、Novaday 的私有儲存空間裡：

  • 你的日記與文字內容。
  • 你附加到日記的照片與影片。
  • 大略位置（需你同意）。只用來標示一篇日記的地點或地區，並與該篇日記一起儲存。Novaday 不會在背景追蹤你的位置。
  • 你的密碼（如有設定）。只保存密碼的雜湊值，存放在 iOS 鑰匙圈（Keychain）或 Android Keystore 中，密碼本身從不儲存。
  • App 設定和一份小型的本機錯誤紀錄，只在你選擇送出意見回饋時用來顯示診斷資訊。

我們收集的資料

沒有。Novaday 沒有帳號系統，也沒有自己的伺服器，不會將你的日記、媒體、位置或任何使用資料傳送給開發者或第三方。App 內沒有任何分析、廣告、追蹤或當機回報 SDK。

備份

你可以把日記匯出成 .zip 備份檔。這個檔案在你的裝置上產生，只有在你自己選擇儲存、匯出或分享時才會離開裝置，例如存到「檔案」、iCloud 雲碟、透過 AirDrop 或其他 App。之後檔案的去向由你和你選擇的服務決定。

意見回饋

從 App 送出意見回饋時，Novaday 會打開你自己的郵件 App 或 GitHub 的問題回報頁面，並預先填好草稿。在你親自送出之前不會傳送任何內容，你也可以先檢查或修改訊息。草稿可以附上基本診斷資訊（App 版本、系統版本、語言、螢幕尺寸、日記數量和最近的錯誤訊息），送出前可以關閉這個選項。你送出的訊息由你的郵件服務商或 GitHub 依其隱私權政策處理。

權限

只有在你使用需要的功能時，Novaday 才會請求取用照片、相機或位置；只有在你開啟提醒時，才會請求通知權限。提醒是在你的裝置上排程的。你可以隨時在裝置設定中變更這些權限。

刪除你的資料

你的資料只存在你的裝置上，由你掌控。你可以在 App 內刪除日記；刪除 App 會一併移除它在裝置上的資料。已匯出的備份檔不受影響，可在你存放的位置自行刪除。

兒童

Novaday 不會收集任何人的個人資料，包括兒童。

政策變更

如本政策有所變更，新版本會公布在此頁面，並更新生效日期。

聯絡方式

對隱私有任何疑問，歡迎到 github.com/dominicshing/novaday-pages/issues 開一則問題回報。
